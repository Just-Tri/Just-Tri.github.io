Welcome to the Smokin' BBQ Hub

BBQ can be made in many different ways. Some people like smokin' meat for hours for that smoky flavor, while others enjoy grilling with a side of BBQ sauce or using the oven. Here we don't judge. You can learn about different BBQ cooking methods and find recipes to try at home today.

BBQ Cooking Styles

Smoking

Smoking uses low heat, a lot of time, and of course a smoker. The smoker slowly cooks the meat while adding that smoky flavor.

Grilling

Grilling gets the job done quickly. It usually uses high heat and cooks over the flame. This method is great for steaks, burgers, and hot dogs.

Indirect Heat

Indirect cooking keeps food away from direct flames and cooks it more slowly. This works well for larger cuts of meat.

Oven BBQ

The oven is a great way to cook BBQ indoors if you do not have a smoker or grill. It uses controlled heat and works well for ribs and chicken.

BBQ Tips for Beginners

  • Season your meat before cooking.
  • Preheat your grill before using it.
  • Use a thermometer to check the temperature.
  • Let meat rest before cutting it.
