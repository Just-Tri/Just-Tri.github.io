[ Air Fryer Sirloin Steak Sandwiches & Bacon Jam ]

[ Prep: 15 mins | Cook: 20 mins | Servings: 2 ]

[ Ingredients ]

For the Bacon Jam:

  • 6 slices thick-cut bacon, chopped
  • 1 medium onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 2 tbsp water
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

For the Steak & Sandwiches:

  • 1 large sirloin steak (10-12 oz), patted dry
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 crusty ciabatta or hoagie rolls
  • 2 cups fresh arugula or spinach
  • 1 ripe tomato, sliced
  • 1 tbsp balsamic glaze

For the Garlic Aioli:

  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • A squeeze of lemon juice
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

[ Instructions ]

  1. Make Bacon Jam: Sauté chopped bacon until crispy. Leave 1 tbsp grease, sauté onions until soft, add garlic, then simmer with bacon, brown sugar, vinegar, and water until jammy.
  2. Cook Steak: Preheat air fryer to 400°F. Rub steak with oil, salt, and pepper. Air fry for 8-12 mins, then rest for 5 mins.
  3. Prep Aioli & Greens: Mix mayonnaise, garlic powder, lemon juice, salt, and pepper. Toss arugula with olive oil and balsamic glaze.
  4. Assemble: Toast rolls, spread garlic aioli, layer with sliced sirloin, tomato, dressed arugula, and a thick spread of warm bacon jam. Serve!
