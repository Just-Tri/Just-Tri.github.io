[ Cajun-Spiced Crispy Tilapia Sandwich ]

[ Prep: 15 mins | Cook: 10 mins | Servings: 2-4 ]

[ Ingredients ]

For the Fish & Batter:

  • 2-4 tilapia fillets (patted dry)
  • 1 cup tempura batter mix (or flour/cornstarch blend)
  • 1 tbsp Cajun seasoning blend
  • 1/2 to 2/3 cup cold water
  • Cooking oil (for shallow frying)
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

For the Slaw & Spread:

  • 1-2 cups shredded cabbage or coleslaw mix
  • 1 lime (juiced and zested)
  • 1 tsp sugar (for the slaw)
  • 6 tbsp mayonnaise

For Assembly:

  • 2 soft demi-baguettes or brioche buns, split and toasted
  • Optional: Fresh lettuce or tomato slices

[ Instructions ]

  1. Make the Slaw: Toss coleslaw mix with sugar, juice of half a lime, salt, and pepper. Refrigerate.
  2. Make Lime Mayo: Combine mayonnaise with remaining lime juice, zest, and a pinch of salt. Chill until ready.
  3. Mix Batter: Whisk tempura mix, half the Cajun seasoning, cold water, and salt into a smooth pancake-like batter. Season fillets.
  4. Fry the Tilapia: Dip fillets into batter, let excess drip off, and shallow fry in hot oil for 2-3 minutes per side until crispy.
  5. Assemble: Spread lime mayo on toasted buns, layer with crispy Cajun tilapia and crunchy lime slaw. Serve immediately!
