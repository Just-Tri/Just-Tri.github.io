[ Garlic-Herb Grilled Pork Chop Sandwich ]

[ Prep: 15 mins | Cook: 10 mins | Servings: 4 ]

[ Ingredients ]

For the Herb Marinade & Sauce:

  • 1/2 cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1/4 cup fresh lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp fresh parsley, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp fresh basil, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp fresh oregano
  • 1 tbsp fresh mint, finely chopped
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

For the Sandwiches:

  • 4 boneless pork chops (pounded to even thickness)
  • 4 soft Italian rolls or brioche buns
  • 1 medium sweet yellow onion, sliced into rings
  • 2 tbsp butter (for cooking onions)
  • Optional: Lettuce, tomatoes, garlic-herb mayo

[ Instructions ]

  1. Make the Marinade: Whisk together olive oil, lemon juice, vinegar, garlic, and herbs.
  2. Marinate the Pork: Reserve 1/4 cup of marinade. Pour the rest over pork chops and refrigerate for at least 4 hours.
  3. Caramelize Onions: Sauté sliced onions in butter over medium heat for 8-10 minutes until soft and golden. Set aside.
  4. Grill or Sear: Cook pork chops for 4-5 minutes per side until fully cooked (145°F). Rest for 3 minutes.
  5. Assemble: Place pork chops on toasted rolls, top with caramelized onions, and drizzle with reserved fresh herb sauce.
